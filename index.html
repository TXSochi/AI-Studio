The studio

We are art directors who work with models, not around them. A machine can hand you a thousand options before lunch. Knowing which one is right, and being able to say why, is still the job.

Twelve people across two cities: art directors, a film editor, two machine learning engineers and one very patient producer.

How a project moves

  1. 01

    Listen

    We start with what the brand must never look like. Then the archive, the references and the people who will live with the work.

  2. 02

    Train

    We build a private model on your visual language and test it until its mistakes are ones we can predict and correct.

  3. 03

    Generate

    The model makes hundreds of directions. Our art directors cut them down to a handful worth arguing about with you.

  4. 04

    Refine

    Retouching, type, sound and edit are done by hand. Nothing ships until a person on our team has signed it off.
